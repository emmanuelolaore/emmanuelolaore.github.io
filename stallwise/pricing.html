Common questions

Do I need a computer?

No. Stallwise works in your phone browser, so you can record a sale or check stock from the shop counter.

Can I import my current product list?

Yes. Upload a spreadsheet or CSV with product name, price and stock, and Stallwise builds your catalogue in a few minutes.

What happens when my trial ends?

You move to Starter automatically. Nothing is deleted, and you only pay if you choose a paid plan.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer in naira. Yearly plans are invoiced once.
